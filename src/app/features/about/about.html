Beyond the technology list

I take growth seriously, without expecting it to happen all at once.

Most of my progress has come from showing up consistently, being willing to ask questions, working through what I do not understand and trying again with a clearer approach. Curiosity helps me look deeper; patience and responsibility help me stay with the work when the answer is not immediate.

I value feedback because it reveals what I cannot always see on my own. I want to leave systems clearer than I found them, adapt when the evidence changes and help other people make progress without pretending I already know everything.
